  • Institute of Questionable Neuroclarity Jul 2026 A fake research institute that diagnoses your vibes and bills you for them. Itemised receipt included; existential VAT is 20%.
  • A Gentle Tour of SFC May 2026 Structural–functional coupling, made interactive without dumbing it down. How brain wiring predicts brain activity.
  • Stage-Linkage & Its Limits May 2026 A visual essay on dopamine and alpha-synuclein across Parkinson's and schizophrenia. There is an easter egg. Nobody has to find it.
  • Which Brain Region Are You? May 2026 A personality quiz with real neuroanatomy underneath and a deadpan on top. You are, probably, the amygdala.
